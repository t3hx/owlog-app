|---|---|---|---|
| `chakra-petch-600.woff2`, `chakra-petch-700.woff2` | Chakra Petch | Cadson Demak | OFL 1.1 |
| `ibm-plex-sans-var.woff2` | IBM Plex Sans | IBM / Mike Abbink, Bold Monday | OFL 1.1 |
| `jetbrains-mono-var.woff2` | JetBrains Mono | JetBrains | OFL 1.1 |
